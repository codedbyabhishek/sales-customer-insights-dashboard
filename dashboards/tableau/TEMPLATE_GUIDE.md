# Tableau Dashboard Template Guide

## Worksheets
- Net Sales Trend (Line)
- Profit by Category/Subcategory (Bar)
- Segment vs Region (Highlight Table)
- Channel-Campaign Performance (Heatmap)
- Cohort Retention (Table + Color)

## Dashboard Structure
- Header: KPI summary
- Left: trend and profitability visuals
- Right: customer behavior visuals (RFM + cohort)

## Filters
- Month
- Region
- Segment
- Channel
- Campaign
