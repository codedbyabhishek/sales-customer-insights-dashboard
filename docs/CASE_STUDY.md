# Case Study: Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard

## Business Problem
Sales and marketing teams lacked a unified view of customer value, campaign impact, and product profitability.

## Objective
Build a decision-ready analytics system to improve targeting accuracy and budget allocation.

## Data Scope
- 20,000 customers
- 450 products
- 180,000 orders
- Dimensions: segment, region, channel, payment method, campaign, order status

## Solution
- Built data model and transformation pipeline in Python.
- Created KPI marts for BI consumption (Power BI/Excel/Tableau).
- Implemented RFM scoring, cohort retention, and profitability breakdowns.
- Added SQL analytics pack for ad hoc business questions.

## Key Outcomes
- Higher confidence in campaign-channel mix decisions.
- Clear visibility into high-value segments and retention trends.
- Faster stakeholder reporting via pre-aggregated marts.

## Portfolio Talking Points
- Demonstrates advanced data modeling + business analytics.
- Shows end-to-end workflow from raw data to executive dashboards.
- Highlights SQL depth with CTEs and window functions.
